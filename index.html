Research & Case Studies

Published Research

Feast on the Desperate

Presented August 2025

The Challenge: Analyzes the systematic exploitation of human vulnerabilities in cryptocurrency fraud, focusing on parasocial dynamics, FOMO, and deceptive social engineering vectors.

Methodology: Synthesizes behavioral indicators with the SCAMS checklist, the I-PACE cognitive model, and technical prevention mechanics to engineer the Crypto Scam Assessment & Mitigation (CSAM) framework.

Key Findings: Proves that threat actors purposefully exploit social trust barriers that bypass traditional technical warnings, necessitating a hybridized psycho-technical defense strategy.
