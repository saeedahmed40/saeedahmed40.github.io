Feast on the Desperate

Research Paper Overview | Presented August 2025

The Challenge

This research addresses the escalating threat of cryptocurrency fraud by examining the social engineering and cognitive exploits targeted at retail investors. It highlights that scams succeed by manipulating psychological triggers such as FOMO and perceived parasocial trust rather than relying purely on technical exploits.

Methodology & The CSAM Framework

Synthesising elements from phishing prevention taxonomies, the SCAMS Checklist, and the I-PACE cognitive framework, the study develops a comprehensive scam mitigation framework integrating psychological markers directly with network-level technical controls.
