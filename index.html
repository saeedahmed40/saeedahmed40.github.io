Research

Feast on the Desperate

Release Date: 15/08/2025

The Challenge: This paper addresses the escalating threat of cryptocurrency scams by examining the social engineering and psychological tactics that exploit individuals. It identifies a critical gap in public awareness and shows how social media, through platforms, influencers and targeted advertising, is a primary vector for these schemes. The scams succeed not just through technical means but by preying on human vulnerabilities such as FOMO (fear of missing out) and other emotional manipulation.

Methodology & Analysis: Through analysis of scam victimisation and real-world testimonies, the work examines why individuals fall prey to these schemes. It synthesises phishing prevention, the SCAMS Checklist, the I-PACE model and insights from parasocial relationships to develop a new, comprehensive framework for scam prevention.

Key Findings & Contributions: Scammers systematically exploit financial fears and parasocial trust to bypass logical decision making. The primary contribution is a framework that gives individuals a combined set of tools to identify and avoid fraudulent cryptocurrency schemes, integrating psychological and social factors with established technical prevention methods.

Implications & Conclusion: Understanding the psychological tactics scammers use helps individuals and organisations resist them. The framework is a resource for targeted educational initiatives and preventive measures against cryptocurrency scams.

Metaverse & Exposure of Children to the Dark Corners of the Internet

Status: Currently researching

The metaverse, a growing network of persistent, immersive virtual worlds, presents new challenges for child safety. Platforms such as VRChat offer unprecedented social interaction, but their open and often unmoderated nature can expose minors to harmful content, grooming and simulated sexual assault. This research investigates the vulnerabilities of such platforms, analyses how harm occurs, and evaluates technological and policy-based safeguards for children.

Code Red for Healthcare: Why the NHS is Losing the War on Ransomware

The NHS is under constant operational strain, and that fragility is amplified by a relentless wave of cyberattacks. Ransomware can cripple hospital operations, compromise patient data and put lives at risk.

This white paper argues the problem is not only funding but a technological foundation that relies on high-maintenance operating systems, creating an attack surface that is too large to defend. It proposes a move to a secure-by-design, Zero Trust architecture, exemplified by ChromeOS.
